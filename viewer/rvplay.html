Keys: arrows move and turn, Ctrl or F fires, Space or E opens doors, Shift runs, < > strafe, Tab map, Esc menu, Enter and Y answer it. A tapped key stays down until two frames later. The buttons latch: click once to hold a key down, again to let go. Each frame is computed by about 23,000 NAND gates stored as folders and takes two or three minutes.
